A person in the organisation, by email address, with their role. Applying a Member for someone who is not yet in the organisation sends them an invitation, the same one the members page sends; the object then reports phase Pending, with the invitation's expiry, until they sign in and accept it. A role change on a member takes effect at once; on a pending invitation it replaces the invitation. Deleting the document revokes an outstanding invitation, or removes the member: their account is deactivated and kept for the audit trail, and they lose access to the organisation. Applying needs an owner or admin. An apply that would leave the organisation without an owner, or remove the person applying, is refused.

## Applying this kind

- **Command.** `vulnetix config apply -f <file>`; the CLI line above says from which release. Plan first with [`vulnetix config plan`](/platform-iac/cli/plan/): it shows each object as create, update, delete or no change, with a `planHash` that apply then holds to. Apply writes in one transaction.
- **Manager.** Each object records the manager that applied it (`terraform`, `console` or `gitops:<repo>`). `--prune` deletes only objects its own manager owns. See [The manager](/platform-iac/cli/plan/#the-manager) and the [config engine](/platform-iac/reference/engine/).
- **Status.** Phase `Pending`, with `invitationExpires`, until the invitation is accepted; then `principalUuid`. The phases the engine reports are explained under [status phases](/platform-iac/reference/engine/#status-phases).

## How apply treats it

- Applies the members page's writers and its role rules (`internal/orgroles`). A new member is an invitation: the status phase is `Pending` with `invitationExpires` until it is accepted. A role change on an invitation revokes and re-invites.
- The member's email is the object's identity. An undeclared member exports under a name derived from the email, and a document for an existing email adopts that person (plan warns).
- Plan blocks: a caller below admin, granting owner as an admin, granting admin, an email change, two documents for one email, and any plan that leaves no owner. Apply refuses removing the person applying.
- Every Authentik write registers its undo for an apply that does not commit.
- Delete revokes the invitation, or deactivates the principal (kept for the access log) and strips its groups.
